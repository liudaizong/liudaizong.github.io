I am Daizong Liu (刘岱宗), a final-year PhD candidate supervised by Prof. [Wei Hu](https://www.wict.pku.edu.cn/huwei), at Wangxuan Institute of Computer Technology of Peking University (PKU). Prior to joining PKU, I obtained my M.S. degree from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST).

My broad research interests include Multimodal Reasoning, 3D Point Cloud Representation, Adversarial Robustness, and Large Vision-Language Models. I have published several papers in top conferences such as NeurIPS, SIGIR, CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, ACM MM, EMNLP, AAAI, ICDM and top journals including TPAMI, TMM and TCSVT.
